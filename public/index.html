Frequently Asked Questions

Is VoiceOver Studio completely free?

Yes — no signup, no credit card, no usage limits. Generate as many voiceovers as you need.

Which voices are available?

60+ Microsoft Edge Neural TTS voices. English (Andrew, Ava, Brian, Jenny), Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean and more.

Can I download the audio?

Yes. Every voiceover downloads as a high-quality 24kHz MP3 file you can use anywhere — YouTube, podcasts, reels, presentations.

What is the script length limit?

Up to 10,000 characters per generation — roughly 5–7 minutes of audio, enough for a full YouTube video script.

What is Andrew voice?

Andrew (en-US-AndrewNeural) is Microsoft's most natural-sounding male English voice. It is warm, clear, and professional — ideal for YouTube and podcast content.

Does it work for YouTube?

Yes. VoiceOver Studio is built for YouTube creators, podcasters, and content creators who need a realistic AI voiceover without expensive subscriptions.
